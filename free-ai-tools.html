Free AI Tools for Everyday Work

AI tools can help with writing, research, planning, brainstorming, and many everyday tasks. You do not need to use lots of tools at once. Start with one tool that solves a real problem and learn how to check its results.

1. AI Chat and Writing Assistants

Useful for: Drafting, brainstorming, explaining concepts, outlining, and rewriting text.

Examples include ChatGPT and Google Gemini.

Check the current free-plan limits, features, and privacy settings on each service before use.

2. AI Research and Information Search

Useful for: Exploring a topic, finding possible sources, and preparing questions for further research.

Perplexity is one example of an AI-assisted search service. Always open sources and verify important claims yourself.

3. AI Design and Visual Content

Useful for: Creating design drafts, social media graphics, and visual ideas.

Canva offers design features, including AI-assisted options. Availability may depend on your account and plan.

4. Prompt Templates You Can Copy

Use these prompts in an AI tool and replace the bracketed text with your own details.

Writing Assistant Prompt

Help me write a clear and useful [type of content] for [target audience]. The main goal is [goal]. Use a [tone] tone and organize the result with a clear heading and short paragraphs. Do not invent facts. Ask me for missing information if necessary.

Research Planning Prompt

Help me research [topic]. Suggest key questions, important terms, and types of reliable sources to consult. Separate established information from questions that need verification. Do not invent citations or claim that you checked a source unless you actually did.

Workday Planning Prompt

Help me organize my tasks for today. I have [number] hours available. My tasks are [list tasks and deadlines]. Prioritize the most important work, include reasonable breaks, and create a realistic schedule. Tell me if the workload is too large for the available time.

Content Idea Prompt

Suggest 15 useful content ideas for [business or topic], aimed at [audience]. Include tutorials, frequently asked questions, common mistakes, and practical tips. For each idea, explain the problem it solves. Avoid repeated ideas and unsupported claims.

How to Choose the Right Tool

  • For writing: Choose a tool that helps you draft and revise text.
  • For research: Choose a tool that helps you find and inspect sources.
  • For design: Choose a tool that matches the content format you need.
  • For productivity: Choose a tool that fits your workflow and saves measurable time.

Important Things to Check

  • Whether the service currently offers a free plan.
  • Daily or monthly usage limits and any paid features.
  • How the service handles submitted prompts and uploaded files.
  • Whether you have permission to use generated content commercially.
  • Whether outputs need fact-checking or editing.
Safety tip: Never enter passwords, payment details, private customer information, or confidential documents into an AI tool unless you understand the risks and have permission to do so.
