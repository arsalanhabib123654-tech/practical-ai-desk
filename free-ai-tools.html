Free AI Tools and Prompts

Practical prompts to help you plan work, write emails, and organize content.

Copy a prompt below and paste it into your preferred AI assistant. Replace the information in brackets with your own details.

1. Daily Work Planner

Organize your tasks and decide what to do first.

Act as a practical productivity assistant. Help me plan my workday. My tasks: [List your tasks] My available time: [Hours available] My most important deadline: [Deadline] Create a realistic schedule with priorities, estimated time for each task, and short breaks. If I have too many tasks, help me choose what to do first.

2. Client Email Writer

Draft a clear and professional email for a client.

Act as a professional business communication assistant. Purpose of the email: [Purpose] Recipient: [Client or colleague] Important details: [Add details] Tone: Friendly, professional, and clear Write a concise email with a suitable subject line. Do not invent facts or promises. Use placeholders where information is missing.

3. Content Calendar Planner

Create a simple content plan for a website or social media page.

Act as a content planning assistant. My topic or niche: [Topic] My audience: [Target audience] My platform: [Website, LinkedIn, Instagram, etc.] How often I can publish: [Posts per week] Create a 4-week content calendar in a table. Include the date or week, topic, content format, main idea, and a useful call to action. Suggest original, helpful ideas and avoid repeating the same topic.

4. Article Outline Builder

Turn a topic into a structured outline before writing.

Act as an educational article editor. Article topic: [Topic] Target reader: [Reader] Main purpose: [Purpose] Create a clear article outline with an introduction, useful headings, practical examples, and a conclusion. Identify questions the article should answer. Do not invent statistics, sources, or quotations.

Tip: Review AI-generated content for accuracy, originality, and suitability before publishing or sending it to a client.
